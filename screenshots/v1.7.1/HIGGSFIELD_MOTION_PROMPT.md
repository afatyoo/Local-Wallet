## Master Higgsfield Prompt

Create a premium 38-second motion graphic product film for FinanceApp, a private personal finance tracking application. Use the supplied screenshots as exact source frames. Preserve the original dark interface, emerald green highlights, red expense accents, typography, English labels, IDR currency values, charts, tables, and spacing.

Open on the FinanceApp login screen in a dark studio environment. Use a slow cinematic push-in while the wallet icon emits a subtle emerald pulse. Transition into the dashboard with a smooth interface reveal. Animate summary cards with restrained upward motion, count the financial values into place, and draw the income-versus-expenses bars and category chart with clean data visualization motion.

Move through Income and Expenses using a controlled horizontal camera glide. Highlight realistic transactions such as Monthly Salary, E-commerce Integration, Apartment Rent, Home Internet, Weekly Grocery Shopping, and Emergency Fund Contribution. Open the Add Expense dialog with a precise scale-and-fade animation. Transition to the receipt preview using a paper-scan light sweep that feels professional and secure.

Continue through Bills and Budget. Animate paid status indicators, budget progress bars, and category limits without changing any source data. Transition into Savings and Savings Targets with subtle progress-ring animation. Show Spending Insights, the Expense Heat Map, and Financial Health Score with smooth chart drawing and a clear score reveal.

Enter the Financial Planning workspace. Move between Net Worth, Debt and Receivables, Category Rules, Notifications, and Activity History using elegant tab transitions. Emphasize Total Assets, Total Liabilities, Net Worth, debt progress, smart categorization, email alerts, and auditable activity.

Finish on Reports, Settings, and User Management. Briefly emphasize PDF and Excel reporting, encrypted backups, two-factor authentication, MySQL storage, English language support, and administrator controls. End by returning to the FinanceApp dashboard as the interface settles into focus. Fade in the final title: "FinanceApp" followed by "Personal finance, clearly organized." Add a subtle emerald light sweep and fade to black.

Camera movement must remain smooth and restrained: slow push-ins, small parallax shifts, clean pans, and precise UI-focused zooms. Use seamless match cuts based on cards, charts, and navigation positions. Keep all text sharp and readable. The final result should feel trustworthy, private, practical, modern, and designed for everyday financial tracking.
